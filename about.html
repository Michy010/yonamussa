About Dr. Yona J Mussa

Dr. Yona J Mussa is a distinguished Dental Surgeon, the 19th President of the Tanzania Dental Surgeons Association (TDSA), and the Founder and Director of the Kinywa Salama Initiative. With a passion for improving oral health care in Tanzania and beyond, Dr. Mussa has dedicated his career to clinical excellence, public health advocacy, and leadership development in the dental profession.

Public Speaking & Events

As a recognized leader in dental health and public health advocacy, Dr. Yona J Mussa is available for speaking engagements, conferences, workshops, and media appearances. His talks combine clinical expertise with practical insights on leadership and community health solutions.

Speaking Topics

The Future of Oral Health in Africa

An evidence-based look at current challenges and innovative solutions for improving dental care access and quality across the continent.

Leadership in Healthcare

Lessons from serving as TDSA President and leading the Kinywa Salama Initiative through periods of growth and challenge.

Community-Based Health Solutions

How grassroots initiatives can create sustainable impact when combined with policy-level changes.

Dental Education & Workforce Development

Strategies for building capacity in oral health professionals to meet population needs.

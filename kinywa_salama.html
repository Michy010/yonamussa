About Kinywa Salama

Founded by Dr. Yona J Mussa in [year], the Kinywa Salama Initiative (which translates to "Safe Mouth" in Swahili) is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving oral health awareness, access to care, and education in Tanzania's most underserved communities.

Our Mission

To create sustainable solutions for oral health challenges through community engagement, education, and accessible dental care services.

Our Programs

School Oral Health Program
